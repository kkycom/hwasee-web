6. Cookies, browser storage, and advertising

The English pages of the Service group cookies and similar browser storage into two categories. The first is always active; the second runs only after you accept it.

6.1 Strictly necessary storage (always active)

This is storage the Service cannot work without. It is not used for advertising or measurement, and it is kept even if you reject the second category:

  • Sign-in state, so you are not logged out between visits. On the English pages this is held in your browser's local storage (hwasee_user_id, hwasee_token, hwasee_nickname) rather than in cookies.
  • Your cookie choice itself (hwasee_en_cookie_consent), so the banner does not ask you again on every page.

6.2 Analytics and advertising (only after you accept)

Until you press Accept on the cookie banner, analytics and advertising storage stay switched off:

  • Google Analytics (GA4). The English pages use Google Consent Mode with ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, and analytics_storage all set to denied by default. In that state the Google tag still loads and may send a cookieless, anonymous signal to Google, but it does not read or write analytics or advertising storage on your device. Pressing Accept switches those four settings to granted.
  • Kakao AdFit advertising. AdFit is not covered by Google Consent Mode, so it is controlled by not loading it at all: the AdFit script is added to the page only after you press Accept. Before that, no request to AdFit is made.

6.3 What Accept and Reject do

  • Accept — grants the four Consent Mode settings above and loads the AdFit advertising script. Your choice is saved in this browser.
  • Reject — leaves analytics and advertising storage denied, loads no advertising script, and removes the empty advertising slots from the page. Your sign-in state is unaffected.
  • No choice yet — treated exactly like Reject until you decide.

Your choice is stored only in the browser you made it in. If you use another browser or device, or clear your site data, the banner will ask again. This policy page itself loads no analytics or advertising scripts.

Your current choice on this browser: checking…

Resetting clears the saved choice and reloads this page, so the banner appears again and you can pick Accept or Reject. It does not sign you out.

  • Google privacy & terms: policies.google.com/privacy
